# Privacy Policy for Tally Counter – Click Counter

**Effective date:** May 2, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how **Tally Counter – Click Counter** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Data Collection

We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from you. The App does not require you to register, log in, or provide any personal information.

However, we use a third-party analytics service, **Amplitude**, to help us understand how users interact with the App and improve its performance. Amplitude may automatically collect the following types of data:

- Device information (e.g., model, OS version)
- App usage data (e.g., screen views, session duration)
- Anonymous identifiers (e.g., device ID, IP address)

This data is collected in **aggregated and anonymous form** and is not linked to your identity.

---

## 2. How We Use Data

We use the data collected by Amplitude to:

- Improve the app’s functionality and performance
- Understand user behavior to guide development
- Detect bugs and crashes

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use **Amplitude** for analytics. Their privacy policy is available at:

## 4. Data Retention

We retain anonymous, aggregated usage data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above.

## 5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data collected by the App using secure methods provided by Amplitude. However, no system is completely secure.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

This App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If such data is discovered, it will be promptly deleted.
